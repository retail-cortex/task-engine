Produce Freshness Inspection & Shelf Rotation SOP

Document Ref: SOP-PROD-001 | Version 1.0

1. Hourly Inspection Routine

Produce associates must conduct temperature checks and shelf rotation checks every hour.

  • Inspect produce displays for visual blemishes, culls, or damage.
  • Log display chiller temperatures in GORM. Limits: 34°F to 38°F.
  • Cull overripe, soft, or molded items and discard in appropriate cages.

2. FIFO Rotation Protocol

Implement First-In, First-Out (FIFO) rules for replenishment.

  • Pull older stock to the front, placing new stock at the back/bottom.
  • Verify price markdown tags for items within 24 hours of expiration.
